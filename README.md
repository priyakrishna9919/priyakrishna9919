## 🙋‍♂️ About Me

I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer with 3+ years of experience embedding AI and cloud-native solutions directly into client environments. I architect agentic AI systems, design RAG and multi-agent pipelines, and deliver production-grade Python and TypeScript backends on AWS and GCP.

From Fortune 500 retail clients to university EdTech platforms — I take messy enterprise problems and ship working solutions, fast.

- 🤖 I build multi-agent AI systems using LangGraph, LangChain, and LlamaIndex

- ☁️ I live in AWS Lambda — serverless is my comfort zone

- 🔌 I speak fluent MuleSoft, REST, OAuth 2.0, and enterprise integrations

- 🏆 I go from discovery workshop to working POC in a single sprint

- 📍 Based in Cleveland, Ohio — shipping code that actually runs in prod
